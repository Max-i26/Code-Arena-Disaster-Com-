## 🎯 Overview

This guide provides a comprehensive, step-by-step testing walkthrough to manually verify all **6 stages**, all **4 stakeholder personas**, the **5-check AI pipeline**, and the **automated outcomes** of the ResQCity system.

## 📝 Step-by-Step Manual Walkthrough

### Test Case 1: Ingesting Citizen Hazard Report (Full 6-Stage Pipeline)
1. Navigate to the top navigation bar and click **Citizen Portal**.
2. Select **Report Hazard**.
3. Click the preset **🌊 Deep Road Flood (Severe)** (or choose *Kelani River Basin*, enter `Baseline Road (A1)`, and select *Flooding*).
4. Check the box **"Are you trapped or in need of immediate evacuation?"** and enter household count: `3`.
5. Click **Submit Hazard to ResQCity Engine**.
6. **Expected Outcome**:
   - The *Instant Pipeline Diagnostic* card appears immediately.
   - Verdict is **`CONFIRMED`** with confidence $\ge 85\%$.
   - **Reasoning chain** explicitly highlights: image water depth, river sensor correlation, and arterial highway priority.
   - Status changes to **⛔ ROAD CLOSED** on the live public GIS map.
   - Council ticket is generated, and area alert is broadcasted.

---

### Test Case 2: AI Photo Spam & Meme Rejection Test
1. In the **Citizen Portal**, click the preset button **🐱 Non-Hazard / Joke Meme**.
2. Enter description: `"Just a test joke photo"`.
3. Click **Submit Hazard to ResQCity Engine**.
4. **Expected Outcome**:
   - Image AI flags non-infrastructure artifacts.
   - Verdict is **`REJECTED`** with low confidence score ($< 15\%$).
   - The public map does NOT close any roads, and false reporter score is penalized.

---

### Test Case 3: Pre-emptive Storm Telemetry & Autonomous Sensor Trigger
1. Locate the **Storm Stream Replay Engine** bar at the top of the screen.
2. Click the phase button **17:00 Flash Flood (Peak)** (or click *Next Storm Phase*).
3. Observe live rainfall gauges spiking to $65-88\text{ mm/h}$ and river ultrasonic sensors climbing to $94\%$.
4. **Expected Outcome**:
   - An automated system case is generated by the *Autonomous Telemetry Watchdog*.
   - A pre-emptive area warning is broadcasted across Ward 02 prior to citizen hotline complaints.

---

### Test Case 4: Council Officer Review & Squad Dispatch
1. Switch to **Council Control** on the top navigation bar.
2. Filter cases by **Kelani River Basin** or **CRITICAL Only**.
3. Click **Inspect 5 Checks & AI Rationale** on any active case.
4. Review the modal displaying all **5 discrete checks** (2 SYSTEM in cyan + 3 AI in purple).
5. Close the modal, find the pending ticket in the right column, select **Rapid Flood Pump Unit 01**, and click **Dispatch Emergency Squad**.
6. **Expected Outcome**:
   - Work order status updates to **`DISPATCHED`**.
   - Field crew position updates and receives the job order.

---

### Test Case 5: Field Crew Resolution with Photographic Proof
1. Switch to **Field Crew** on the navigation bar.
2. Select the dispatched crew from the dropdown.
3. Observe the **Safe Navigation & Detour Instructions** routing the truck around flooded road sectors.
4. Review the completion photo URL (pre-filled with verified clean road photo).
5. Click **Complete Job & Clear Public Map**.
6. **Expected Outcome**:
   - Work order updates to **`RESOLVED`**.
   - The hazard marker turns green on the GIS map, and the road is marked **OPEN**.
   - Crew unit returns to **`AVAILABLE`** standby.

---

### Test Case 6: Relief Desk & Intelligent Shelter Allocation
1. Switch to **Relief & Shelters** on the navigation bar.
2. Review the live shelter capacity grid showing occupancy bars and emergency food/water supplies.
3. Under *Pending Evacuation Requests*, locate the stranded family request.
4. Click **Auto-Match Nearest Available Shelter**.
5. **Expected Outcome**:
   - System matches the family to the closest shelter with remaining capacity (e.g. *St. Peter's College Community Hall*).
   - Shelter bed occupancy increments automatically, and food/water rations are reserved.
   - A safe detour route avoiding submerged roads is rendered on the map.

---

### Test Case 7: System Admin & AI Continuous Learning Loop
1. Switch to **Admin & AI Tuning** on the navigation bar.
2. Adjust the **AI Confidence Cutoff Slider** from $70\%$ to $85\%$ and click **Save AI Configuration**.
3. View the **Stage 06 Continuous Learning Feedback Log** displaying previous officer confirmations and dynamic prompt adjustments.

## 🧪 Automated Postman API Execution

You can run the full automated API test suite using Postman or curl:

```bash
# 1. Health check
curl -X GET http://localhost:3001/health

# 2. Get state
curl -X GET http://localhost:3001/api/state

# 3. Submit 6-Stage Hazard Report
curl -X POST http://localhost:3001/api/reports \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "userId": "citizen-101",
    "userName": "Saman Kumara",
    "hazardType": "FLOOD",
    "severity": "HIGH",
    "lat": 6.958,
    "lng": 79.891,
    "roadName": "Kelani Embankment Road",
    "imageUrl": "https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1547683905-f686c993aae5?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80",
    "description": "Rising floodwater over curb, 3ft deep",
    "needsRescue": true,
    "householdCount": 4
  }'

# 4. Resolve Work Order with Photo
curl -X POST http://localhost:3001/api/tickets/ticket-02/resolve \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
```
